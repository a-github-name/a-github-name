I help product teams make consequential technical decisions — and ship the systems behind them. My path runs from film and games, through multi-million-dollar U.S. Department of Defense research at USC, to enterprise AI and CTO leadership.

Today I run **[Sawfwair](https://sawfwair.com)**, a founder-led software practice based in Prince Edward Island and working globally. I serve growing teams as a fractional CTO while building **[Mere](https://mere.run)**: a local-first creative AI ecosystem spanning inference, workflow authoring, private remote compute, and agent-ready operations.
